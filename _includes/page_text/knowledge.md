First, the docs related to computing and data science.

   - Algorithms: Linear Regression -- Classification *Naive Bayes, Logistic Reg, Maximum Likelihood* -- Decision Trees -- SVM -- KNN -- KMeans -- DBSCAN -- Reinforcement Learning -- Feature Selection / Dimensionality Reduction -- Association Rules -- Neural Networks
   - Cases: Recommender Systems

 - [**GDocs Statistics**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LGm6v2gzczZt8pEkbRh_W5SvcERKFtT-3Uoz54ILXzA/edit?usp=sharing) here you can learn how to look at data and analyze it to extract hidden information and patterns.
